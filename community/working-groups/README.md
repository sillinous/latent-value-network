# LVN Working Groups

The Latent Value Network is built by five working groups, each responsible for a domain of the protocol. You don't need to be an expert to join — you need to be willing to do the work.

## The Groups

| Group | Focus | What You'd Work On | Looking For |
|-------|-------|--------------------|-------------|
| **AI Integration** | MCP, A2A, implementation | MCP server, A2A agents, relay, taxonomy packages | Engineers with MCP/A2A experience, Python/TypeScript developers |
| **Privacy & Cryptography** | Signal privacy, ZK proofs, threat models | Differential privacy tuning, Bloom filter analysis, privacy audits | Cryptographers, privacy engineers, security researchers |
| **Theory & Foundations** | Mechanism design, network science | Complementarity function, incentive analysis, taxonomy structure | Researchers in network science, economics, mechanism design |
| **Ethics & Equity** | Red-teaming, equity, governance | Adversarial review, equity metrics, consent frameworks, deployment review | Ethicists, community organizers, policy researchers, anyone affected |
| **Community & Practice** | Pilots, practitioner input, outreach | Pilot community engagement, onboarding materials, feedback collection | Community organizers, social workers, educators, librarians |

## How to Join

1. **Read** the relevant specifications (start with [WD-001](../docs/WD-001-white-paper.md), then the documents your group owns)
2. **Open** an [Introduction issue](https://github.com/sillinous/latent-value-network/issues/new?template=introduction.md) telling us who you are
3. **Pick** the group(s) that match your skills and interests
4. **Show up** — contribute to discussions, review PRs, or build components

Not on GitHub? Email [latentvaluenetwork@proton.me](mailto:latentvaluenetwork@proton.me) with your background and interests.

## Decision Making

Within-group decisions: rough consensus among group members (see [WD-006](../docs/WD-006-governance-framework.md)).
Cross-group decisions: Stewardship Council (all group leads).
Equity concerns: Ethics & Equity has advisory veto on deployments.

## Current Status

All groups are forming. The first priority is identifying leads and establishing meeting cadence. See [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) for the full development timeline.
