# LVN Community Charter

**How We Make Decisions Together**

*Derived from [WD-006: Governance Framework](../docs/WD-006-governance-framework.md)*

---

## Core Commitments

1. **Transparency.** Every decision is documented with rationale and dissent.
2. **Affected parties decide.** People impacted by a decision participate in making it.
3. **No permanent authority.** All roles have term limits. The founding contributors will relinquish any special status.
4. **Ethics & Equity veto.** Deployments that fail equity review do not proceed.
5. **Forkable.** Anyone can fork the protocol. This is the ultimate check on governance.

## Decision Types

### Specification Changes
- **Proposed via:** GitHub Discussion or Pull Request
- **Review by:** Relevant working group
- **Comment period:** 14 days (28 days for protocol-level changes)
- **Accepted by:** Rough consensus — no strong unaddressed objections

### Taxonomy Changes
- **Subcategory additions:** 7-day comment period, approved by Theory & Foundations
- **Category additions:** 14-day comment period, approved by Theory & Foundations + Community & Practice
- **See:** [TAXONOMY-GOVERNANCE.md](../specs/taxonomy/TAXONOMY-GOVERNANCE.md)

### Pilot Decisions
- **Proposed by:** Community & Practice working group
- **Requires approval from:** Ethics & Equity (consent and equity review)
- **Must pass:** Privacy audit (WP 2.5 in PLAN.md)

### Community Policy
- **Proposed via:** GitHub Discussion
- **Comment period:** 14 days
- **Accepted by:** Rough consensus of all active contributors

## What "Rough Consensus" Means

We follow the IETF model: a decision is accepted when there is broad agreement and no strong, unaddressed objections. This does NOT mean everyone agrees — it means objections have been heard, addressed with evidence or argument, and remaining disagreements are acknowledged.

"No strong objection" ≠ "everyone is happy"
"No strong objection" = "everyone has been heard and no one has a blocking concern that hasn't been addressed"

## When Consensus Fails

1. Extended discussion period (28 additional days)
2. Structured debate with each position given equal time
3. Formal vote among relevant group members
4. Two-thirds majority for protocol changes, simple majority for parameters
5. Decisions passing by less than two-thirds: 90-day cooling period before implementation

## Emergency Decisions

For security vulnerabilities or active exploitation:
1. Any two working group leads can invoke emergency authority
2. Temporary mitigation deployed immediately with public documentation
3. Full governance process begins within 48 hours
4. Emergency actions expire after 30 days without normal approval

## Contribution Recognition

All contributors are recognized in CONTRIBUTORS.md. Contribution includes: code, specifications, analysis, testing, community organizing, critique, and pilot facilitation. There is no hierarchy of contribution types.
