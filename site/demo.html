What if the help you need
is already nearby?

Every day, millions of cooperative connections fail to happen — not because people are unwilling, but because neither party knows the other exists. This is the Latent Value Gap. Let us show you.

👩‍🔧
Maria, 62
Retired mechanical engineer · Newton, IL
Maria retired two years ago. She volunteers at the library and tends her garden. She's been feeling a bit purposeless since retirement — her grandkids live far away and she misses solving problems.
✦ Latent value: 35 years of drainage and HVAC expertise. Fixed her own septic system twice. Could diagnose most residential infrastructure problems in an afternoon.
invisible connection
👨‍👧
James, 34
Single dad, warehouse worker · 3 miles from Maria
James has been noticing water pooling in his yard near the foundation. He Googled it once but the contractor quotes were $3,000+. He figured he'd just deal with it. He doesn't know it's slowly damaging his home's foundation.
✦ Latent value: James rebuilt a deck last summer and is a skilled woodworker. Maria's library needs new bookshelves — a project James would actually enjoy.

This connection died in silence.

Maria could diagnose James's drainage problem in 10 minutes and save him thousands. James could build the bookshelves Maria's library needs. Neither searched for the other. Neither knew they should.

This is the Latent Value Gap. And it happens millions of times every day.

Now imagine an AI that could sense these connections — not by surveilling people,
but by understanding the situations they're already sharing with their AI assistant.

The research says...

83.4% of jobs are found through occasional or rare contacts, not close friends (Granovetter, 1973). Cross-class friendships are the single strongest predictor of upward economic mobility (Chetty et al., 2022). Knowledge workers spend 2.5 hours per day searching for information that someone nearby already has (IDC). The connections that matter most are the ones nobody thinks to look for.

This doesn't exist yet. We're building it.

The Latent Value Network is an open collaborative initiative designing the protocol layer that could make ambient cooperation real. We need cryptographers, economists, ethicists, engineers, and practitioners.
