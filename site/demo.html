The research says...

The people who change your life are almost never the ones you'd think to ask. 83.4% of jobs come through acquaintances, not close friends — people on the periphery of your world who bridge to entirely different ones. 42% of professionals have expertise their own colleagues don't know about. We're surrounded by help we can't see, carried by people who don't know they're carrying it. This isn't a market failure. It's a perception failure. And it's solvable.
