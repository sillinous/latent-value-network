## Production Notes


This script tells the story of the Latent Value Gap through two real-feeling characters — Maria and James — then zooms out to the systemic insight. The animation style should feel warm and human, not corporate or techy. Think of the visual language of Kurzgesagt’s educational videos crossed with Headspace’s gentle aesthetic. The color palette should match the LVN brand: deep navy, warm teal, muted gold, cream backgrounds.
The script is divided into six scenes totaling exactly 2:00. Each scene includes voiceover text (word count calibrated for natural speaking pace of ~150 wpm), visual direction, on-screen text elements, and transitions. The final scene includes three variant CTAs depending on deployment context.

## Word Count & Timing Notes


Note: Pacing runs slightly above standard 150 WPM because animation provides visual support that allows faster narration without losing comprehension. For a slower, more contemplative read, extend to 2:30 by adding 2-3 second pauses between scenes and allowing visual beats to land.

| SCENE 1: THE INVISIBLE PROBLEM  [0:00 – 0:25] |
| --- |
| VOICEOVER Maria is 62. She retired two years ago from a career in mechanical engineering. She volunteers at the library. She tends her garden. Most days, she feels... a little purposeless. Three miles away, James is staring at water pooling in his yard. He Googled it once. The contractor quotes were three thousand dollars. He figured he’d just deal with it. He doesn’t know it’s slowly destroying his foundation. |
| VISUAL Open on a quiet, illustrated neighborhood from above — soft watercolor style. Camera slowly descends. We see Maria in her garden, a gentle smile but slight loneliness in her posture. Pan three houses over to James, looking at standing water with concern. His daughter tugs at his hand. Split screen: both in their separate worlds. A thin, invisible dotted line connects them — but neither can see it. |
| ON SCREEN "3 miles apart"  |  "Neither knows the other exists" |
| TRANSITION: Dotted line between them begins to glow faintly → |


| SCENE 2: THE HIDDEN EXPERTISE  [0:25 – 0:45] |
| --- |
| VOICEOVER Here’s what nobody sees. Maria spent 35 years solving drainage problems. She could diagnose James’s issue in ten minutes — and it probably doesn’t need three thousand dollars. It needs a Saturday afternoon. And James? He rebuilt a deck last summer. He’s a skilled woodworker. Maria’s library has needed new bookshelves for months. They need each other. They just don’t know it. |
| VISUAL Camera zooms into Maria — transparent overlay shows her expertise glowing around her like a warm aura: pipes, blueprints, drainage diagrams, swirling gently. Then zoom to James — his aura shows woodworking tools, lumber, bookshelves. The auras pulse gently. Pull back to the split screen: the auras are reaching toward each other across the houses, but can’t quite connect. |

| SCENE 3: THE GAP AT SCALE  [0:45 – 1:05] |
| --- |
| VOICEOVER Maria and James aren’t unusual. This happens millions of times, every day, everywhere. The retired nurse who could help a neighbor navigate a terrifying diagnosis. The college kid who builds websites for fun while the food bank next door drowns in spreadsheets. The farmer with idle equipment while a family down the road can’t afford to start a garden. Researchers call this the Latent Value Gap. It’s the largest pool of untapped cooperative potential in the world. |
| VISUAL Camera zooms out rapidly from Maria and James’s neighborhood. Now we see hundreds of illustrated people — each with glowing auras of different colors representing their latent capabilities. Dotted lines flicker between dozens of them — connections that could exist but don’t. The density of missed connections is visually overwhelming. Text fades in as the camera reaches a bird’s eye view of a whole community. |
| ON SCREEN "The Latent Value Gap"  |  "$1.3 trillion in untapped cooperative potential" (animated counter) |

| SCENE 4: WHY IT EXISTS  [1:05 – 1:25] |
| --- |
| VOICEOVER This isn’t a motivation problem. People want to help. It’s an information problem. You can’t search for what you don’t know you need. James doesn’t know he needs a drainage engineer. Maria doesn’t know she IS one — not in a way she’d ever put on a profile. No marketplace, no social network, no job board can find this match. Because neither party would ever think to search for it. |
| VISUAL Abstract space. Three icons appear and cross out: a search bar (X), a social media profile (X), a job listing (X). Each fails to capture the connection. Then: a warm light emerges — an AI assistant gently glowing, sitting alongside a person. It’s observing, not surveilling. It notices patterns in conversation and behavior. It understands context that no profile could capture. |
| ON SCREEN "You can’t search for what you don’t know you need."  |  "✘ Marketplace  ✘ Social Network  ✘ Job Board" |
